❓ How It Works

The basics
  1. Put it on your home screen. iPhone: open in Safari, tap Share ⬆︎, then Add to Home Screen. Android: in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen or Install.
  2. Tap in from the start screen.
  3. Use the bar at the bottom: 🏈 Football or 🏀 Basketball schedule, ⚡ jump to the next game, 🍔 Tailgate or 📺 Watch Party invites, and 🔥 Trash Talk.
  4. Tap any game to open its preview and details.
📅 Schedule & games

The countdown at the top always shows the next game. Each game card shows date, time, TV network and where to stream it. Tap a card to see the preview and prediction.

Use the chips to show All Games, Home, Away, or Marquee matchups. The progress bar tracks how far into the season we are, and the record box keeps the W–L tally.

🔴 Live scores & rankings

The Scoreboard card loads the latest UCF score. Tap ↻ to refresh during a game; it turns red while a game is live.

Tap Rankings & Standings to open the AP Top 25 or the Big 12 standings. UCF's row is highlighted.

📆 Add games to your calendar

Find the Add games to your calendar card on each sport's schedule. Subscribe once (iPhone/Mac Calendar or Google Calendar) and new kickoff and tip-off times show up on their own as they're announced.

🎉 Watch parties & tailgates

Hosting

  1. Tap 🍔 Tailgate (football tab) or 📺 Watch Party (basketball tab) in the bottom bar.
  2. Pick 📺 Watch Party or 🍔 Tailgate, then the game.
  3. Where: pick 🏠 My house, 🏡 A friend's house, ⭐ one of your usual spots, 🍺 A bar near me (we list the closest ones), 🅿️ the stadium lot, or ➕ somewhere new. Add the address or tap 📍 Here to drop a map pin where you are. Check Save as a usual spot to make it one tap next time.
  4. Add the meet time, your name and a note, then tap Create & Send Invite and pick your group text.

Your friends tap the link (no app or sign-up), type their name and pick 🙌 I'm in (and how many), 🤔 Maybe or 😞 Can't. Tailgate guests can say what they're bringing. Afterward they can add it to their calendar and tap Invite a friend; people who answer from a forwarded link show as "Sue via Dave."

Keeping track: your parties are listed at the bottom with live counts. Tap Who's coming for names (you can remove an answer with ×), and Send again the day before as a reminder.

Tailgate food & gear checklist: at the bottom of the tailgate screen. It's saved on your phone; Share Plan sends it to the crew.

📣 Share Hype & 🔥 Trash Talk

Share Hype writes a countdown message ("6 days until UCF vs…") and opens your phone's share menu.

Trash Talk serves up a one-liner for the group chat. Tap 🎲 for another, 📋 to copy it.

⚙️ Settings & buttons
  • 🌙 / ☀️ switches dark mode.
  • ✕ goes back to the start screen.
  • ? opens this guide.
🔒 Privacy

No accounts or sign-ups. The app counts how many phones open it, using a random ID (no names or personal info). Your tailgate lists stay on your phone. Watch party invites and answers are saved on the invite service so the host and friends can see them.

💛 Support

The tracker is free, ad-free and made by a Knight fan. If you like it, tap Chip in near the top of the schedule (or Support this app at the bottom) to send a tip on Venmo.
