❓ How It Works

The basics
  1. Put it on your home screen. iPhone: open in Safari, tap Share ⬆︎, then Add to Home Screen. Android: in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen or Install.
  2. Tap in from the start screen.
  3. Use the bar at the bottom: 🏈 Football or 🏀 Basketball schedule, ⚡ jump to the next game, 🍔 Tailgate (football) or 📺 Watch Party (basketball), and 🔥 Trash Talk.
  4. Tap any game to open its preview and details.
📅 Schedule & games

The countdown at the top always shows the next game. Each game card shows date, time, TV network and where to stream it. Tap a card to see the preview and prediction.

Use the chips to show All Games, Home, Away, or Marquee matchups. The progress bar tracks how far into the season we are, and the record box keeps the W–L tally.

🔴 Live scores & rankings

The Scoreboard card loads the latest USF score. Tap ↻ to refresh during a game; it turns red while a game is live.

Tap Rankings & Standings to open the AP Top 25 or the American standings. USF's row is highlighted.

📆 Add games to your calendar

Find the Add games to your calendar card on each sport's schedule. Subscribe once (iPhone/Mac Calendar or Google Calendar) and new kickoff and tip-off times show up on their own as they're announced.

🍔 Tailgate planner (football)

On the football tab, tap Tailgate and pick a home game.

  • Who's Coming? Add names and how many each person brings. The headcount totals at the bottom.
  • Who's Bringing What? A starter list (burgers, buns, cooler, grill, chairs, cornhole…). Check items off, edit them, add your own, and put a name next to each.
  • Share Plan turns it all into one text you can send the crew.

The tailgate plan is saved on your phone only. Share it to keep everyone in the loop.

📺 Watch Party (basketball)

For hoops, there's no tailgate, so throw a watch party at a bar or someone's house.

  1. On the basketball tab, tap Watch Party.
  2. Pick the game and fill in where, the address, what time to meet, your name and an optional note (like "wings half off").
  3. Tap Create & Send Invite and text the link to your friends.

Your friends tap the link (no app needed) and answer 🙌 Meet you there (with how many are coming), 🤔 Maybe, or 😞 Can't make it. They can change their answer any time and get a map button to the spot.

Forwarding: anyone can tap Invite a friend to pass it on. People who answer from a forwarded link show as "Sue via Dave," so you can see how word spread.

As the host, your parties are listed at the bottom of the Watch Party screen with live counts. Tap Who's coming to see everyone's answers. You can remove an answer with ×.

📣 Share Hype & 🔥 Trash Talk

Share Hype writes a countdown message ("6 days until USF vs…") and opens your phone's share menu.

Trash Talk serves up a one-liner for the group chat. Tap 🎲 for another, 📋 to copy it.

⚙️ Settings & buttons
  • 🌙 / ☀️ switches dark mode.
  • ✕ goes back to the start screen.
  • ? opens this guide.
🔒 Privacy

No accounts or sign-ups. The app counts how many phones open it, using a random ID (no names or personal info). Your tailgate lists stay on your phone. Watch party invites and answers are saved on the invite service so the host and friends can see them.

💚 Support

The tracker is free, ad-free and made by a Bull fan. If you like it, tap Chip in near the top of the schedule (or Support this app at the bottom) to send a tip on Venmo.
